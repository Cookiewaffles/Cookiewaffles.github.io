My Story

I'm Jacob Heiser, a .NET Software Devloper currently working with the State of Michigan to help enrich the lives of people in my State. My studies first started in Grand Rapids Community College where I earned my Associate's Degree in Computer Science. Afterwards, I joined Full Sail University where I earn a Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science on a accelerrated learning curve. Before joining the State, I participated in a Boot Camp to polish my skills that gave me the oppurtunity to make connections and work with the State of Michigan as a developer.

Ever since I was young, I look for ways to improve the lives of the others around me. Orginally, that led me down the path of Game Development. To create games of such quality that they could provide a break from the normal stresses of life to the people who played them. After college, I came to the conclusion that the turnover rate was too high and I wouldn't be able to provide the level of impact I was striving for. Then the oppurtunity with the State came, and I knew that my goals could be alcomplish here.

During my time working for not just a degree but a work place that aligned with my goals, I have work on many personal projects on my free time to improve my skills. Several different games using the Unity Engine and applications in various languages. And even with this change in career paths, I still work in Game Design as a hobby with my friends to produce a game made by us.
